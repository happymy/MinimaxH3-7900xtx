正面提示词中的“隐形排除”写法
既然没有负面框，你需要用正面、可观测的约束句来表达“不要什么”。关键区别在于：

无效写法（没人接收）	有效写法（正面提示词内）
no blur	Frame edges are razor-sharp with zero softening.
no extra people	Exactly one person is visible in the frame.
no noise	Surface textures render as clean, defined detail without digital artifacts.
no text	The frame contains no captions, no logos, and no on-screen text.
原则：用“什么存在”来描述“什么不该存在”。H3 对“Exactly one person”的遵循度，远高于一个孤立的 no extra people。

📋 可嵌入的正面修复模块
把以下内容直接粘贴到你现有提示词的末尾，替换掉上一轮我给你的一切负面内容。

模块 A：场景契约（放在提示词最前面）
text
[Scene Contract]
One subject only. The subject occupies the central 40% of the frame. 
Wardrobe, appearance, and identity are fully specified and remain consistent for the entire clip. 
The environment is defined by concrete physical objects, not by mood words.
为什么有效：H3 是 CFG-distilled 模型，它没有“被排斥”的路径，只能“被吸引”到明确的视觉锚点上。你越精确地锁定画面内容，模型自由发挥（即制造模糊和噪点）的空间就越小。

模块 B：画质锁定（贴在场景描述之后）
text
[Quality Lock]
Ultra-sharp native 768p output. Tack-sharp focus on the subject with edge-to-edge clarity.
Visible micro-textures: fabric weave, skin pores, surface grain, material details.
No motion blur on static elements. Hard-edge boundaries between subject and background.
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, 35mm lens. High-frequency detail retention across the entire frame.
Restrained film grain only (replace with "Clean digital rendering, no film grain" if you want pure clarity).
关键：No motion blur on static elements 是正面约束，它告诉模型“静止的东西必须清晰”，这比说“不要模糊”更有效，因为它在描述一个可观测的物理状态。

模块 C：排除块（放在提示词最后，用正面句式）
这是 H3 社区验证的替代方案——用 1-2 句可观测的约束句结尾，而不是一长串负面词表。

text
[Frame Constraints]
Exactly one person in frame. No captions, no logos, no on-screen text.
The camera is locked or moving at a single, constant speed. 
The composition is clean and uncluttered, with no competing elements.
根据你的具体问题，可以替换第一句：

如果常出现多余人物：Exactly one person is visible, fully specified, occupying the central portion of the frame.

如果常出现文字：The frame contains no text of any kind, including signage, captions, or watermarks.

如果画面杂乱：The composition contains only the named subject and the specified environment elements. No additional objects appear.

🚫 仍然需要从你提示词中删除的词
这些词无论在正面还是负面位置，都会导致 H3 启用全局平滑策略，直接制造发虚：

删除	原因	替代
soft lighting	触发全局柔化	Broad diffused illumination from a large source, with readable skin texture
dreamy / 梦幻	全局平滑	Clean rendering with defined edges
atmospheric haze	雾面感	Clear air with visible depth cues
beauty / flawless / 完美皮肤	塑料皮肤	Natural skin texture with visible pores and micro-detail
cinematic blur	全画面模糊	Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the subject
🧪 如果边缘仍然发虚：边界强化专项
把以下句子逐字加在动作描述之后：

text
The subject boundary maintains hard-edge separation from the background. 
No anti-aliasing softness. Fabric weave visible. Hair strands individually resolved. 
Surface reflections show specular highlights with defined edges.
同时在“Frame Constraints”块中追加：Frame edges are razor-sharp with zero softening.
